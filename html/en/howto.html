How to Place an Order

Step 1 — Contact Us

Please use our contact form or call us to enquire about products, pricing, and availability. For international orders, please include your country and desired quantity.

Step 2 — Receive a Quote

We will send you a quote including product price, packaging options, international shipping cost, and estimated delivery time.

Step 3 — Payment

Once you confirm the order, we will provide payment instructions. International bank transfer (TT) is available.

Step 4 — Shipping

We ship via Japan Post EMS or international courier (FedEx / DHL) depending on destination and package size. Tracking information will be provided.

Returns & Exchanges

Returns are accepted only in the case of defects or errors on our part. As tororo kombu is a perishable food product, we cannot accept returns for reasons of personal preference. Please contact us within 7 days of receipt if there is an issue.
