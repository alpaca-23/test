About Tairou Kombu

Founded in 1980 (Showa 55) by the Masaki family in Hikone, Shiga Prefecture — home of the National Treasure Hikone Castle — Tairou Kombu (Meiwa Corporation) has been specialising in the production and direct sale of premium tororo kombu for over 40 years.

We source only the finest Donan Shirokauchi-hama kombu (kelp from the southern Hokkaido coast), process it through our traditional 8-step method, and deliver it fresh-shaved to customers across Japan and beyond.

Our tororo kombu is shaved to just 0.02mm — thinner than a human hair — by precision machinery, giving it a uniquely light, fluffy texture and intense umami depth.
02

The Name "Tairou"

The name Tairou (大老) carries historical significance. In the Edo period, Ii Naosuke — Lord of Hikone Domain — served as Tairou (Senior Regent), the highest office in the Tokugawa shogunate. By taking this name, our shop honours Hikone's proud heritage and commits to the same level of dedication and quality.

Just as the Tairou commanded the highest standard, we hold our kombu to the highest standard of freshness, flavour, and craft.

03

Tororo vs Oboro Kombu — What's the Difference?

Both are shaved kombu products, but they differ in production method and texture:

Comparison
Oboro Kombu (おぼろ昆布)

Shaved from the flat surface of a single sheet of kombu by hand. A craft technique seen at department store demonstrations. Produces wide, thin, translucent sheets.

VS
Tororo Kombu (とろろ昆布)

Shaved from the side edge of a pressed block of layered kombu by machine. Produces fine, fluffy strands with consistent quality — our specialty at Tairou Kombu.

Our tororo kombu is made using the machine method across 8 carefully controlled production steps, resulting in a uniform, high-quality product with exceptional umami flavour.
