Welcome

What is Tororo Kombu?

🫙
Japanese Umami Superfood
Tororo Kombu (とろろ昆布)

Tororo kombu is made by shaving pressed kombu kelp into ultra-thin, fluffy strands. Rich in natural glutamates (umami), iodine, and dietary fiber, it's used in soups, rice balls, hot pots, and more. Vegan & gluten-free.
